Governance is infrastructure

01 / OPERATING MODEL

The rules should ship with the system.

01

Collect for a defined purpose

Deployments begin with an explicit mission, approved signals, and visible limits—not open-ended collection.

02

Process close to the source

Edge-first perception reduces unnecessary data movement and keeps useful decisions available when networks degrade.

03

Make every action reviewable

Searches, exports, policy exceptions, and operator actions produce a traceable record for authorized oversight.

04

Keep authority with people

The system can surface evidence and recommend next steps. People remain accountable for consequential decisions.

A product with boundaries

Useful intelligence.
Deliberate limits.

VULK is designed to

  • Detect defined public-safety events from approved sensor feeds
  • Enforce deployment-specific retention and access policy
  • Work locally when cloud connectivity is constrained
  • Give operators and oversight teams a shared audit trail

VULK is not designed to

  • Create a limitless, centralized archive by default
  • Hide searches, exports, exceptions, or operator actions
  • Make consequential enforcement decisions autonomously
  • Treat privacy and civil liberties as policy paperwork
